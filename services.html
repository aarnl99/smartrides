The Business Case

Shuttle programs work for employers and employees

Employee shuttle programs solve two problems at once: they take pressure off recruiting and retention, and they hand employees back time and energy a solo commute takes away.

National surveys have found that up to 23% of employees have left a job over an unworkable commute, and the EPA estimates a shared commute program saves roughly 4.6 tons of CO2 a year for every vehicle it takes off the road.

Employer Benefits

  • Employees arrive at work without the stress of driving in traffic or hunting for parking
  • A visible perk that helps you attract and retain talent, especially with employees under 35
  • Cuts down on the parking and real estate a growing campus needs to build or lease
  • Reduces your company's carbon footprint as part of a broader sustainability goal

Employee Benefits

  • Relax, read, or catch up on rest while someone else handles the drive
  • Get a head start on email or admin tasks before you're even in the building
  • Skip the stress of navigating traffic, tolls, and parking on your own
  • Ride in newer, well-maintained vehicles built for a comfortable daily commute

Common Questions

What companies ask before switching

How quickly can service start?

Timelines depend on route complexity and vehicle availability, but most programs launch within a few weeks of a signed agreement.

Is there a minimum contract length?

Contracts are built to scale with your headcount. We work out terms that fit your program during the proposal stage rather than locking you into a fixed template.

What if our headcount or shift patterns change?

Routes and schedules get adjusted as your business changes. That's handled directly through your dedicated account contact, no need to renegotiate the whole agreement.

Are drivers background-checked?

Yes. Drivers are professionally trained and background-checked before being assigned to any route.
